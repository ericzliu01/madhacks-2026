How to read this. Rows labeled simulated come from a bot whose behavior encodes our hypotheses about the planted flaws: they show the pipeline recovers those effects, not that real users have them. agent rows are bots running the real dashboard in a browser with one fixed strategy for both versions, so differences come from the designs, not from rates we chose; they are still not people. human rows are real sessions. Types ending in _r1 were recorded on the earlier design (hidden assistant in v1, tooltips in both versions) and are kept apart. Samples are small: every rate shows its n, and none of these differences are tested for significance.
